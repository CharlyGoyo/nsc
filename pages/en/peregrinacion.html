What is the NSC Pilgrimage?

Our Lady of Christendom – Spain (NSC-E) is an annual pilgrimage to the shrine of Our Lady of Covadonga (Asturias) organised by a group of faithful lay Catholics devoted to the celebration of the Holy Mass according to the Extraordinary Form of the Roman Rite. It takes place around the feast of St. James the Apostle (July 25th), patron saint of Spain.

our aim

The aim of the pilgrimage is the sanctification of the soul through the graces asked of Our Lord, through the intercession of the Blessed Virgin Mary, by offering Him prayers, sacrifices and mortifications during three days. During these days of pilgrimage we especially commend our Homeland and the Holy Father.

We seek to contribute to the restoration of the spirit of Christendom —according to our possibilities and always with divine help— which has given the Church and the world so many saints, heroes and defenders of the Faith. We refer to the Christian social order, which is only possible if we commit ourselves to the restoration of all things in Christ, beginning with those of us who go on pilgrimage, our families, and the various spheres of society in which we move.

For such a bold endeavour, we place our trust in the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass, the foundation of Christian life. Therefore, an important part of our apostolate is to foster and encourage devotion to the Holy Mass. During the Pilgrimage we seek to highlight and recall the four ends of the Mass:

Adoration, to honour God in the most fitting way

Thanksgiving, to thank God for His immense gifts

Propitiation, for our sins and for the souls in purgatory

Supplication, for our particular needs and those of the world

The Mass is the very Sacrifice of Christ on the Cross, offered by the Son of God to the Eternal Father, to obtain the graces necessary for Salvation. The Church has always taught that the Sacred Liturgy is one of the ways by which the Good Lord communicates His Grace to us, that is, Trinitarian Life itself; it also teaches us the truths of Revelation in a particularly admirable way (lex orandi, lex credendi), for it is inspired by the words and deeds of Our Saviour. With this in mind, NSC-E wishes to make known and loved the Mass celebrated according to the Extraordinary Form of the Roman Rite, whose celebration was favoured and recommended by Pope Benedict XVI’s motu proprio Summorum Pontificum.

We wish to show, therefore, the need to restore the Christian social order through the graces received in the Holy Mass. For all these reasons, NSC-E makes an annual penitential pilgrimage to Covadonga to ask our Mother for the graces needed to carry out this mission.

Who can participate?

Adults Any layperson in good physical condition may take part as a walking pilgrim. Pilgrims walk in groups called Chapters.
Families and children Children aged 6 and over may take part in the pilgrimage. Families and children walk alongside the adults in their respective chapter, following a shorter route adapted to their abilities, covering at most 14 km per day.
Priests and religious Their presence is indispensable to provide spiritual assistance to the pilgrims. They do not need to walk the entire pilgrimage; they may participate even for a single day or along certain stages and exercise their ministry during rest breaks. NSC-E provides all necessary assistance for their transport and provisions.
Volunteers Laypeople who do not walk, yet make the pilgrimage in another way by voluntarily assisting with the logistics and organisation of the Pilgrimage.
