Travelling to Oviedo

The trip to Oviedo is not managed by the Organisation. However, we encourage participants to contact their Chapter Heads to consider the possibility of sharing transport so that the journey is more economical.

Coming back to Oviedo

The Organisation will make buses available to pilgrims to return to Oviedo when the pilgrimage ends in Covadonga. Those who wish to use this service must indicate it at the time of registration.

Route

1st day: Oviedo – El Remediu (32 km)

2nd day: El Remediu – Sebares (32 km)

3rd day: Sebares – Covadonga (27 km)

Family route: about 12-14 km/day

Preparation recommendations

  • Always start at low intensity and increase progressively.
  • Watch out for any muscle discomfort in your feet or elsewhere during the walk. If any part of your footwear or clothing causes discomfort, it is preferable to stop and adjust it.
  • Stay hydrated before, during, and after exercise.
  • Pay special attention to stretching once exercise is finished. If the walk is long, it is also advisable to stretch during a brief break halfway through.
