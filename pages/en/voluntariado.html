Volunteering

What does it consist of?

As a volunteer, you can carry out many tasks: setting up tents, access control, cooking, march safety, preparing rest stops… Volunteers will not walk the pilgrimage on foot (except for march order volunteers and some medical staff).

  • Throughout the day there are breaks to attend spiritual talks, as well as to share snacks with fellow volunteers. You are welcome to bring something to share!
  • All volunteers receive full lunch and dinner as well as the Pilgrim's Book.
  • They can enjoy an exclusive area at the campsite for volunteers and clergy: a space to eat and rest together, with food and drinks.

How do I become
a volunteer?

  • Make sure you meet the requirements.
  • Submit an application in the area of your choice.
  • You will be contacted by the area coordinator for a personal interview.
  • Once accepted as a volunteer by the Organisation, you must formally confirm your registration by paying the registration fee. Once the pilgrimage has ended and you have fulfilled your duties as a volunteer, you may request a refund or leave it as a donation.

Requirements to become
a volunteer

  • Comply with the Pilgrimage Regulations.
  • Willingness to help.
  • Have no physical or psychological impediments for the role you are volunteering for.
  • Be available from the beginning to the end of the Pilgrimage.
  • Have the Telegram app installed on your phone (to coordinate with the Organisation).
  • Specific requirements for each area.

areas

LOGISTICS

Logistics volunteering during the pilgrimage is possibly one of the most demanding roles. It is divided into two distinct areas: supplies and transport.

While the pilgrims walk, you will make sure everything around them runs smoothly so that they can focus on what truly matters: the journey to Covadonga, toward Our Mother.

supplies

  • Warehouse: coordination of the logistics distribution centre from which all the equipment needed for the Pilgrimage is dispatched.
  • Cleaning: it is of vital importance to keep all the spaces we use in a spotless condition.

transport

  • Equipment: includes the transport of all kinds of equipment, as well as setting up the campsite, supplying water, fruit and bread at the rest stops, and managing backpacks.
  • Personnel: transport of injured pilgrims along the route, as well as other transport needs within the Organisation.

Backpack volunteers will be able to join the walk after loading and unloading the bags.

We are looking for people who see logistics not merely as technical work, but as an act of charity and self-giving. If you identify with these values and skills, your contribution will be key to the success of the pilgrimage.

ORDER

Order volunteers are essential to ensure the Pilgrimage proceeds in an orderly manner and that pilgrims know where to go at all times. They are divided into two sections: march and rest stops.

march

They are responsible for guiding the column of pilgrims at the set pace and keeping it together, ensuring proper spacing between chapters.

Age requirement: between 20 and 60 years old.

rest stops

Organises the three rest breaks for pilgrims during the march so that order is maintained at all times and pilgrim flow remains smooth. The team also determines the placement of fruit, water and bread, portable toilets, and rubbish bins.

Age requirement: between 25 and 65 years old.

SECURITY

The security volunteer is responsible at all times for ensuring the smooth running of the pilgrimage, enforcing rules of conduct, carrying out the plan devised by the organisation, and safeguarding pilgrims so that no internal or external threat disrupts the pilgrimage.

campsite and rest stops

Carries out access control, enforces regulations, and performs night watch duties to maintain campsite security. They are responsible for executing the evacuation and emergency plan.

crossings

Controls safety and vehicle access at rest stops and directs traffic at the most dangerous road crossings along the pilgrims' route.

FAMILIES

The families team helps ensure that all pilgrims on this adapted route complete the pilgrimage safely and in an orderly manner, making sure they board and disembark from the buses at scheduled times, guiding them to rest stops, etc.

Requirements:

  • Be physically fit to walk the distances of the family route.
  • Be able to give clear instructions and lead a group with authority and good spirits.

FULL CAPACITY

MEDICAL STAFF

The medical team, composed of professionals from various healthcare fields, is responsible for attending to anyone requiring medical assistance both during the march and at the campsite.

However, each pilgrim must bring their own basic first-aid kit to treat minor injuries, such as small blisters.

CLERGY-CEREMONIES

The Holy Mass is the reason why the Our Lady of Christendom pilgrimage is held. Everything that takes place during it is directed toward Our Lord in the Blessed Sacrament, made present in the Sacrifice of the Holy Mass. For this reason, on each day of the pilgrimage, a Solemn Mass and many private Masses are celebrated.

sacristy

Everything required to prepare for the Holy Sacrifice is arranged by the liturgy volunteers: setting up and dressing the altars, vestments, communion rails, liturgical consumables… In short, making the place worthy for God's coming upon the altar.

infrastructure setup

Setting up tents for private Masses and the sacristy, benches… as well as setting up camp beds for priests staying overnight in shared tents.

clergy hospitality

They attend to the practical needs of priests, such as transporting the liturgical items they carry and ensuring they receive proper provisions.

liturgy

These are specific volunteer roles necessary for the proper celebration of liturgical functions: sound technician, altar server coordinator, communion coordinator, or choir coordinator.

CAMPSITE

Everything must be ready to welcome pilgrims at their resting place at the end of the day, a task carried out by the campsite volunteers.

kitchen

They prepare meals for priests and religious. In addition, they prepare breakfast and a hot broth at night for all pilgrims.

welcome & reception

They mark out and signpost the entire campsite. They welcome pilgrims, answer their questions, and provide the necessary guidance for all participants.

MEDIA & COMMUNICATIONS

The media and communications team is responsible for the audiovisual coverage of the pilgrimage, using both photography and video equipment. It is also in charge of the physical merchandise stall and live updates on social media.

Requirements:

  • Professional experience in any of the fields within this area will be valued, as sensitive equipment and media will be handled.
  • For the merchandise stall, good people skills and efficiency in stock management are required.
