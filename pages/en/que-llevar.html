What to bring?

To see the items that the organisation provides, click here. Below is detailed the gear that each pilgrim needs to bring:

FOOTWEAR

  • Comfortable and sturdy footwear (do not wear new shoes).
  • Optional: moisturiser, petroleum jelly…

CAMPING GEAR

  • Sleeping bag and sleeping mat.
  • Tent (to be coordinated with the members of your chapter). Pilgrims travelling from outside mainland Spain are not required to bring one.

SMALL PILGRIMAGE BACKPACK

This backpack will be used for walking. It should contain:

  • Food for the day.
  • Energy snacks: cereal bars, chocolate, nuts, sweets, etc.
  • Raincoat.
  • Warm layer (it can be cold in the morning).
  • Outdoor camping mess kit if needed for your meals (deep plate, cutlery, cup/mug…).
  • Toilet paper.
  • Water bottle.
  • Hat and sun protection.
  • First-aid kit with medication, blister treatment supplies…
  • Pilgrim’s Book (provided by NSC-E).
  • Personal ID/documentation (do not forget your health insurance card).
  • Rosary.

LARGE BACKPACK

This will be transported by the Organisation along with your camping gear from one campsite to another. It is recommended to pack your belongings in waterproof bags inside the backpack, as bags may get wet if it rains. It should contain:

  • Food that will not be consumed during the day.
  • Dinner for each day.
  • Lightweight spare footwear for the campsite.
  • Spare clothes (do not overpack).
  • Swimsuit (mandatory in the river and “showers”).
  • Personal hygiene items and a lightweight towel. Wet wipes are recommended.
  • Torch / flashlight.
  • First-aid supplies (medical staff will only attend to serious cases).

IMPORTANT

It is essential to label all belongings with your name and your chapter's coloured ribbon (each pilgrim must bring their own ribbon; it is not provided by the Organisation). The colour allocation is listed at the bottom of this page.
Do not tie loose items to the outside of your large backpack; pack everything compactly to avoid loss.

Try to use your mobile phone as little as possible. There will be no power sockets at the campsites to recharge them.
