“Guardian Angels” Chapter

Who are the
guardian angels?

They are all those who, not being able to be physically present during the three days of the pilgrimage, want to make it spiritually. Many would like to do the route but cannot for various reasons: illness, age, professional obligations, distance, etc.

How to be a pilgrim from a distance?

The Church teaches us that, within the communion of saints, “none of us lives for himself, nor does anyone die for himself” (Rom 14:7). “In this way, if one member suffers, all the members suffer with him; and if one member is honored, all the others rejoice together. For you are the Body of Christ and (its) partial members” (1 Cor 12:26-27).

The least of our acts done with charity redounds to the benefit of the other members. Furthermore, united to Christ, “we can merit in favor of ourselves and others useful graces for sanctification, for the growth of grace and charity, and for obtaining eternal life” (CCC 2010).

Thus, through the communion of saints, the prayers and sacrifices made by the “guardian angels”, wherever they may be, and united by charity to Our Lord Jesus Christ, will merit new graces for the column of pilgrims while, reciprocally, those offered by the walkers will attract the graces of the pilgrimage for the “guardian angels”. This reciprocity makes the “guardian angels” true pilgrims to Covadonga.

In these times when Catholicism is so directly attacked, it is urgent to increase and intensify prayer and sacrifice. The prayers of the “guardian angels”, together with those of the column of walkers, will ascend to Heaven imploring mercy, interceding for the Church and for our society, converting hearts and souls, and the graces of the pilgrimage will fall upon everyone.

Who can be a
“guardian angel”?

All those who cannot be physically present during the three days of the pilgrimage.

The “guardian angels” include religious men and women, sick people, people who due to their age cannot walk the journey… In short, all those who are held back by their duties of state or are prevented by their physical condition.

How to make the pilgrimage of the “guardian angels”?

The “guardian angel” pilgrims will have simple, clear commitments adapted to their situation: daily recitation of the Holy Rosary (or some other Marian devotion), and time for meditation (for which we propose following the meditations in the Pilgrim's Book), to which they may add, depending on each person's possibilities: Mass, confession, a work of charity or act of reparation, etc. In addition, in the form at the bottom of the page, you can reserve one or more hours of prayer in which you commit to praying for the Pilgrimage while it takes place.

The “guardian angels” will receive the Pilgrim's Book by email, and thus they will be able to read the same meditations as the walkers. They will pray for the intentions of the pilgrimage and, reciprocally, they can entrust their intentions to the prayers of the walkers.

Locally, they may on their own initiative meet to pray together, either in a church or at home.

If you wish, you can contribute with financial help.
