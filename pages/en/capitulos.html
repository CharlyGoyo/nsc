What is a chapter?

During the pilgrimage, the aim is to maintain a climate of order that facilitates prayer and meditation and also allows the harmonious development of the activities on the schedule.

Therefore, the way to participate in the Pilgrimage is in groups called “Chapters”. These represent natural communities: a region, city, parish, missionary or formation group, school, family, etc., marching together at the feet of Our Mother.

In which chapter do I register?

In order to participate in the pilgrimage it is necessary to be registered in a chapter. If you want to register in a chapter you already know, you have to contact the Chapter Chief (through the e-mail that you will find in the chapter information), who will collect your data and provide you with the necessary information. This does not exempt the requirement to complete the registration form once the term is open, selecting the corresponding chapter.

To those who are interested in attending but do not know any chapter, we encourage you to contact us via email at informacion@nscristiandad.es and we will help you find the most appropriate chapter.

HOW TO CREATE A CHAPTER

Families and children

Children, accompanied by responsible adults, may also participate. They will make a route adapted to their abilities. The recommended age for the families' route is between 6 and 12 years old. Children between 12 and 15 years old will be able to walk the adult or children's route, accompanied by at least one adult family member.

Children under 6 years of age can participate in a different, adapted route—the “little-route”—which is accessible to strollers. They must always be accompanied by a parent. You can find more information here.

Vehicles not affiliated with the organization will not be permitted access to the camps or rest stops.
