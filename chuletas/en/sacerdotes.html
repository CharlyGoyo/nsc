Priests

All interested priests are invited to participate in Our Lady of Christendom – Spain pilgrimage. For them, as well as for men and women religious, registration will be free and, unlike the rest of the participants, they will be provided with lunch and dinner during the days of the march.

Those who have not been able to learn until now to celebrate Holy Mass according to the Extraordinary Form of the Roman Rite are equally welcome, and they will be provided with the necessary resources to learn it. During the three days of the pilgrimage, the Holy Mass celebrated in public will be celebrated in the Extraordinary Form, so this meeting is an excellent opportunity to get closer to it and learn to celebrate it. All priests and seminarians may attend in choir.

The pilgrimage is financed by donations. Registration fees are not enough to cover all the costs. Any donation, smaller or bigger, will be very useful.
